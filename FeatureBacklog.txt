Dynamic Weather System & Live Forecast UI

Concept: Integrate a dynamic weather engine that affects flight routes in real-time.

Mechanics: Routes can experience Storms/Snow (closing the path entirely, forcing drones to wait),
Rain (slowing down transit time), or Tailwinds (speeding up transit).

Visualization: * Render weather icons (clouds, lightning, snow) directly over the affected
connections on the map.

Implement a "Live Weather Alerts" dashboard overlay to display current active storms and closed routes.

2. Infinite Missions & The "Drone Passport"

Concept: Transform the static pathfinding simulation into a continuous, living logistics network.

Mechanics: Once a drone reaches its destination, it doesn't stop. Instead, it dynamically calculates
a new A* path to a randomly generated destination on the fly.

Visualization:

Create a "Fleet Status" dashboard.

Implement a "Passport" system: Every time a drone lands, it collects a "stamp" of that city.

Display a visual "VISITED!" stamp animation over the city when a delivery is completed.

3. Custom Geographical Maps & Asset Rendering

Concept: Move away from abstract nodes to real-world geography (Germany, Europe, Global Maps).

Visualization:

Load high-resolution background maps.

Replace standard dots with specific .png assets for different hubs (e.g., city landmarks, coat of arms,
or ocean tiles) based on the color metadata parsed from the map file.

4. Demographic-Based Hub Capacity (Backend enhancement)

Concept: Tie the maximum drone capacity of a hub to real-world population data.

Mechanics: Update the map parser to read a population= tag. The hub's capacity is mathematically
calculated based on the city's size (e.g., 1 drone capacity per 250,000 residents), making
the simulation highly realistic.
